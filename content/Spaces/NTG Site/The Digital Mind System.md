# The Digital Mind System

## Philosophy

- *Frictionless*
	- Minimising time between thought and action
- *Actionable*
	- Application of thoughts and knowledge gained
- *Intuitive*
	- An extension of the brain, rather than a tool
- *Malleable*
	- Customisable to suit most tasks
- *Accessibility*
	- Access to root files

## Components

### 80/20

#### Obsidian

- Note taking & thought organisation
- Daily notes
- Highlights from pieces of media (book, videos, articles, research papers)

#### Raycast

> Swiss army knife

- Clipboard Manager
- Emoji and Symbol Search and Selector
- Execute shortcuts
- Calculator
- Configurable Hotkeys for opening an application and other functions

**Extensions**
- Pomodoro
- Apple reminders (integration of native reminders app)
- Coffee (to keep computer awake temporarily)
- Color Picker (sample colours in the screen)
- Google search (quickly search google without the need to enter the browser)
- Screen OCR (extraction of text from images on screening)

### 100% All in

#### Homerow

- Navigate and scroll using the keyboard

#### Aerospace

- Tiling Window Manager

#### Karabiner-Elements

- Keyboard Remapping
	- Remapped 'caps-lock' to 'esc' when pressed and 'cmd+opt+crl+shift' when held
