# Broad Ligament

> [!ontology]+
> up:: [[Peritoneum]], [[Female Reproductive System]]
> jump:: [[Ovaries]]
> down:: 

> [!Definition] Definition
> The broad ligament is a fold of [[Cards/Peritoneum]], which is the membrane that lines the abdominal and pelvic cavities. The broad ligament connects the uterus, fallopian tubes, [[Cards/Ovaries]], and vagina on one side to the anterior abdominal wall on the other side. It also contains blood vessels, nerves, [[Cards/Lymphatic Vessels]], and fat cells. The broad ligament is involved in various functions such as supporting the uterus and fallopian tubes, providing space for the [[Cards/Ovaries]] and vagina, and transmitting sensory information from the uterus and fallopian tubes to the brain.
