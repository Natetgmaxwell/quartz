# DNA Replication

> [!Ontology]+
> up:: [[Mitosis]]
> jump::
> down:: 

> [!Definition] Definition
> [[Cards/DNA Replication]] is the process by which the [[Genomes\|Genome's]] [[Cards/Deoxyribonucleic Acid\|DNA]] is replicated to be transferred into the new daughter cell.

## Steps

![[Extras/Images/SXLMIA7K.png]]

> [!cite]-
> [[Sources/Video/@kuballa2024BIM202M02T01MolecularBasis]]

1. [[Cards/Helicase]] unwinds the parental double helix
2. The leading strand is synthesised continuously in the 5'-> 3' direction by DNA [[Cards/Polymerase]].
3. The lagging strand is synthesised discontinuously. [[Cards/Primase]] synthesises a short [[Cards/Ribonucleic Acid\|RNA]], which is extended by [[Cards/Deoxyribonucleic Acid\|DNA]] [[Cards/Polymerase]] to form an Okazaki fragment.
