# Bronchus

> [!Ontology]
> up:: [[Lungs]]
> jump:: [[Bronchioles]]
> down:: [[Cards/Primary Bronchi]], [[Cards/Secondary Bronchi]], [[Cards/Tertiary Bronchi]]

> [!Definition] Definition

![[Bronchus Diagram.excalidraw.svg]]

- [[Primary Bronchi]], also known as the lobule. Each lung has 250 million lobules, each containing a cluster of alveoli surrounded by large and small airways.
- [[Secondary Bronchi]], which are longer and narrower than the proximal ones. They connect the upper to the distal bronchioles and form several subdivisions, such as the segmental branches that correspond to each lung segment on a chest X-ray.
- [[Tertiary Bronchi]], which extend into the alveolar sacs where gas exchange occurs. They are further divided by transverse fissures and vertical ridges into lobule-like units called acinus. Each lung has about 300 million alveoli, each measuring about 1 micrometer in diameter.
