> [!Definition] Definition

- Talking to yourself in the second person (using your name or referring to yourself through the pronoun 'you') can help alleviate self [[Cards/Bias\|biases]] and gain perspective on situations. Distant self-talk lets you think like you're helping someone else and perceiving the situation objectively ([[Sources/Podcasts/Podcast Episodes/You 2.0  Befriending Your Inner Voice]]).

> [!quote] [[Sources/Courses/Scientific Thinking and Communication]]
> "don’t take eyewitness testimony as to the ultimate measure of things, eyewitness testimony is among the least reliable forms of evidence and is maximally susceptible to [[Cards/Bias]]"
