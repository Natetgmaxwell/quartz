> [!Definition] Definition
> [[Cards/Dihybrid Cross]] is a cross between two individuals with two observed traits ([[Cards/Phenotype]]) controlled by two distinct [[Cards/Genes]].

## Example

S = spherical >> s = dented
Y = yellow >> y = green
$$Homozygous Spherical Green *Dent Yellow $$
