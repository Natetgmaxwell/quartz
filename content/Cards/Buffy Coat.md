# Buffy Coat

> [!ontology]
> up:: [[Anti-coagulated Centrifuged Blood]]
> jump:: 
> down:: [[White Blood Cells]], [[Platelets]]

> [!Definition] Definition
> The **buffy coat** is a thin, pale layer in a sample of blood collected after centrifugation, consisting primarily of **white blood cells (leukocytes)** and **platelets (thrombocytes)**
