# Glutamic acid

> [!Ontology]-
> up:: [[Amino Acids]]
> jump:: [[Cards/Memory]]
> down:: 

> [!Definition] Definition
> [[Cards/Glutamic acid]] is an amino acid used to form [[Cards/Protein\|proteins]]. In the body, it turns into [[Cards/Glutamate]]. This is a chemical that helps nerve cells in the [[Cards/Brain]] send and receive information from other cells as a [[Cards/Neurotransmitters]]. It may be involved in learning and [[Memory]].
