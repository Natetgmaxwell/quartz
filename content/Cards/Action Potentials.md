# Action Potentials

> [!ontology]
> up:: [[Membrane Potentials]]
> jump:: [[Sodium]], [[Cards/Potassium]]
> down:: [[Refractory Period for Action Potentials]]

> [!Definition] Definition

- Influx of sodium into the cell causes [[Cards/Depolarisation]], then movement of [[Cards/Potassium]] out of the cell causes [[Cards/Repolarisation]]. 
- [[Cards/Acetylcholine\|ACh]] triggers [[Cards/Action Potentials]]
- **Occur in the [[Cards/Axons]] of [[Cards/Neurons]] and [[Cards/Myocyte\|muscle cells]].**
- Obeys as all of nothing law.
