# Gastrinoma

> [!ontology]
> up:: [[Neuroendocrine Tumors]]
> jump:: [[Gastrin]], [[Peptic Ulcers]], [[Zollinger–Ellison Syndrome]]
> down:: 

> [!Definition] Definition
> A gastrinoma is a rare neuroendocrine tumor that produces excessive amounts of gastrin, a hormone that stimulates the stomach to secrete acid, which can lead to peptic ulcers and other symptoms collectively known as Zollinger-Ellison syndrome.
