# Amylose

> [!ontology]
> up:: [[Starch]], [[Polysaccharides]]
> jump:: 
> down:: 

> [!Definition] Definition
> Amylose is a polysaccharide made up of α-D-glucose units linked by α(1→4) glycosidic bonds. It is one of the two main components of starch, accounting for about 20–30% of starch, with [[Cards/Amylopectin]] making up the rest.
