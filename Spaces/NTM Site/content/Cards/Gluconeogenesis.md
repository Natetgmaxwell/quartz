# Gluconeogenesis

> [!ontology]
> up:: 
> jump:: [[Cards/Glucose]], [[Amino Acids]], [[Lactate]]
> down:: 

> [!Definition] Definition
> [[Cards/Gluconeogenesis]] is a metabolic pathway that generates [[Glucose]] from certain non-carbohydrate carbon substrates.

> [!quote] [[@jamesdinicolantonio2021WinAchievePeak]]
> Glucogenic  amino  acids  can  be  converted  into  [[Cards/Glucose]]  through [[Cards/Gluconeogenesis]],  and  they  include  [[Cards/Alanine]],  [[Cards/Arginine]],  [[Cards/Asparagine]], [[Cards/Cysteine]], [[Cards/Glutamic acid]],  [[Cards/Glutamine]],  [[Cards/Histidine]],  [[Cards/Methionine]],  [[Cards/Proline]],  [[Cards/Serine]]  and [[Cards/Valine]].
