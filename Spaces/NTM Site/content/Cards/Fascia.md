# Fascia

> [!Ontology]
> up:: 
> jump:: [[Hypodermis]]
> down:: [[Cards/Thoraco-lumbar fascia]]

> [!Definition] Definition
> Fascia is a type of connective tissue that surrounds and supports [[Cards/Muscle Tissue\|muscle]], [[Cards/Bone Tissue\|bones]], and organs throughout the body. It is made up of [[Cards/Collagen]], elastin, and other proteins, and it forms a continuous, three-dimensional network that extends from head to toe.
