## Notation

1.  There are two words: [[Cards/Genus]] then [[Cards/Species]]
2.  The [[Cards/Genus]] is written with a capital letter, while [[Species]] is given a lowercase letter
3.  When writing with by hand, the words should be <u>underlined</u>
4.  When typing words should be <i>italics</i>
