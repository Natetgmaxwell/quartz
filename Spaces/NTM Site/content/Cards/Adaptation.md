# Adaptation

> [!Ontology]-
> up:: 
> jump:: [[Competitiveness]], [[Evolution]]
> down:: [[Cards/Structural Adaptation]], [[Cards/Behavioural Adaptation]], [[Cards/Physiological Adaptation]], [[Physiological Adaptation]]

Adaptation allows species to remain [[Cards/Interspecific Competition\|competitive]] in their environment while becoming more efficient.

## 3 Types of Adaptation

1. [[Structural Adaptation]] (e.g. venom, larger [[Cards/Ears]])
2. [[Behavioural Adaptation]] (e.g. hibernation, instincts, [[Cards/The Tree of Knowledge Mutation]])
3. [[Physiological Adaptation]] (e.g. ear sensitivity)
