# Electromagnetic Spectrum

> [!Ontology]

> [!Definition] Definition
> The Electromagnetic Spectrum is the range of all types of [[Electromagnetic Radiation]], from low-frequency radio waves to high-frequency gamma rays, that travel through space and include visible light, microwaves, X-rays, and [[Cards/Ultra Violet radiation]], among others.
