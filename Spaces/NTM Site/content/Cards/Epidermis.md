# Epidermis

> [!Definition] Definition
> Outer layer of the [[Cards/Skin]]

- Comprised mainly of [[Cards/Keratinocytes]]
- [[Skin]] is replaced about every 50 days

## Layers of the [[Cards/Epidermis]]

- [[Stratum Corneum]]
- [[Stratum Lucidum]] (**Only found on the soles of the feet and palms of the hands**)
- [[Stratum Granulosum]]
- [[Stratum Spinosum]]
- [[Stratum Basale]]
