# Gonads

> up:: [[Endocrine Glands]]
> jump:: [[Gametes]]

> [!Definition] Definition
> Gonads are the organs that produce gametes and hormones in animals. In humans, gonads are the [[Cards/Ovaries]] (female) or testes (male). Gonads develop from germ cells in the yolk sac during embryonic development.
