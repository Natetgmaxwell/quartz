> [!Definition] Definition
> A major component of the homeostatic response is the [[Cards/Hypothalamic-Pituitary-Adrenal Axis\|HPA]], an intricate, yet robust, neuroendocrine mechanism that mediates the effects of stressors by regulating numerous physiological processes, such as [[Metabolism]], immune responses, and the [[Autonomic Nervous System|ANS]].
