# Fundus of the Stomach

> [!ontology]-
> up:: [[Cards/Stomach]]
> jump:: 
> down:: 

> [!Definition] Definition
> The fundus of the stomach is the part of the stomach that is located above the gastric cardiac junction and below the esophageal hiatus. It is a rounded, conical or pyriform mass that serves as the origin of the vagus nerve and the blood supply to the stomach.
