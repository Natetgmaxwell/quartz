## Flashcards

What are the four heart chambers, and what are their functions?;;The four heart chambers are the left atrium, right atrium, left ventricle, and [[Cards/Right Ventricle]]. The atria receive blood, and the ventricles pump it. The left side pumps oxygenated blood to the body, while the right side pumps deoxygenated blood to the lungs.
