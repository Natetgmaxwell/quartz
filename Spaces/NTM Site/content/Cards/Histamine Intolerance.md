# Histamine Intolerance

> [!Definition] Definition

## Foods to Avoid

- Fermented Beverages ([[Cards/Wine]], [[Cards/Beer]] and [[Cards/Kombucha]])
- Fermented Foods ([[Sauerkraut]], [[Cards/Vinegar]], [[Cards/Soy Sauce]], [[Kefir]] and [[Cards/Yogurt]])
- [[Cards/Cured Meats\|Cured]] or [[Cards/Smoked Meats]]
- Soured Foods ([[Cards/Sour Cream]], [[Sour Milk]] and [[Cards/Buttermilk]])
- [[Dried Fruit]]
- [[Aged Cheese]]
- [[Cards/Peanuts]]

## Foods to Consume

- [[Cards/Eggs]]
- Low-[[Cards/Gluten]] grains (amaranth, buckwheat, millet, quinoa, sorghum, [[Cards/Teff]] and [[Cards/Brown rice]], [[Cards/White Rice]])
- Fresh Fruit
- Fresh Vegetables
- Leafy Herbs ([[Cards/Parsley]] and [[Cards/Cilantro]])
