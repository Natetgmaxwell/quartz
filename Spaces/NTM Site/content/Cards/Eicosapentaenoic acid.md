# Eicosapentaenoic acid

> [!Ontology]
> up:: [[Cards/Omega−3 fatty acids]]
> jump:: [[Cards/Docosahexaenoic Acid\|DHA]]
> down:: 

> [!Definition] Definition
> [[Cards/Eicosapentaenoic acid\|EPA]] is one of several [[Cards/Omega−3 fatty acids]]. It is found in cold-water fatty [[Cards/Fish]], such as [[Cards/Salmon]]. It is also found in [[Cards/Fish]] oil supplements, along with [[Docosahexaenoic Acid|DHA]]. [[Omega−3 fatty acids]] are part of a healthy diet that helps lower risk of [[Cards/Cardiovascular Disease]].
